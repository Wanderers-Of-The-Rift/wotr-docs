## Design Completed

Three Pillars
- **Shape Your Own Path**

Design open-ended systems where choices matter
Avoid fixed roles and narrow solutions
Support multiple valid playstyles

- **Meaningful Progression**

Growth is earned, impactful, and exciting
Unlocks what players can do and how they engage
Progression changes gameplay capabilities

- **Prioritize Experience**
 
Deliver meaningful play moments
Respect player's time and capacity to learn
Quality over quantity in player interactions

### Release Strategy
- Targeting new release (020) then expanding to newer features
- Progress over perfection guiding principle
