# Lin's Updates
- Thanks to everyone who responded to my messages and for your collaboration.
- PParticipation standards are being enforced better than they were before. 
- Discord roles have been adjusted for some. Those shifted to member only role are still welcome here and remain in overview channels and in social areas. Reengagement and new participation is welcome at any time with a new application. 
- links and socials, and the about channel have been updated in preparation for new member recruitment. 
- Call to the community for help with word of mouth recruitment help. 

## Current Project Focus
- Design Pillars
  - Foundational vision statements to guide features, aim to finalize by Sept 29. Then we will shift experience to player focus. 
- Design Reference Library
  -Central hub for sharing game inspiration using a provided template. 
- Guides Program
  - Guides help new or returning contributors onboard and reengage.
  - Spreadsheet for guide assignments and applications is in progress. Contact Warrengore with questions in the interim.
