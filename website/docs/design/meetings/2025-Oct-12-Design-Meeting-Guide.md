# 🧾 Core Player Experience (Phase 2) [Handout]

Companion guide to help follow along during meeting  
It consolidates all team submissions, merged drafts, and theme groupings discussed so far. It is for allowing contributors to follow along easily during the meeting and prepare for the post meeting consensus poll.


---

## Overview

### In this meeting:
- We’ll review all Player Experience submissions and the merged theme drafts.
- Narrow down to **2–3 final Player Experience Statements** for the forum consensus poll.
- Confirm one representative word per **Verb Theme** and **Emotion Theme** (built from contributor submissions).
- After the meeting, a short forum poll will finalize the top statement, verbs, and emotions for inclusion in the **Game Design Document (GDD)**.


### Primary Goals
- Define how it *feels* to play **Wanderers of the Rift** through a unified Player Experience statement.
- Consolidate and finalize **core verbs** and **core emotions** that represent what players do and feel.
- Capture all contributor input in a transparent, accessible format.
- Prepare for the transition to **Phase 3: Core Loop & Progression.**

### Related Resources

- [Phase 2 : Core Player Experience (Related Task Discussion/Submission Forum Post)](https://discord.com/channels/1374772629298483202/1422619205253206178/1422620025990152334)
- [Notes from Prior Meeting](https://wanderers-of-the-rift.github.io/wotr-docs/docs/design/meetings/Oct-04-Design-Meeting-Notes/)

### Meeting Flow

1. **Welcome & Context** | Overview of goals, quick recap of last session.
2. **Review Merged Player Experience Drafts** | Discuss how they represent the shared vision.
3. **Verb Theme Review** | Themes, each narrowed to one key verb.
4. **Emotion Theme Review** | Themes, each narrowed to one representative emotion.
5. **Finalize & Wrap Up** | Confirm shortlist and prepare for consensus.
