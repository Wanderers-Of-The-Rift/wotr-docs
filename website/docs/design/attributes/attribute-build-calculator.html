How the system works

Points are divided into cycles of 90. Each cycle past the first applies diminishing returns to raw stat contributions but perk effects are never diminished.

A perk unlocks every 9 points in an attribute. In cycle 1 you choose one of two. In cycle 2 the unchosen perk unlocks automatically. Cycle 3 grants no new perks.
