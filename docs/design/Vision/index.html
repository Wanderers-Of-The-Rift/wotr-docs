Vision Document

What This Is​

Wanderers of the Rift is a multiplayer-first ARPG built inside Minecraft. The game is about expeditions into rifts; entering emergent dimensions, growing through what you find there, and returning to a hub that evolves with you. The world asks questions it never answers. What players make of it is their own. Rifts are ephemeral by nature. They exist to be explored, not solved.

Pillars​

Pillars define the core of the experience. Every system, feature, and piece of content should reinforce at least one pillar.

Shape Your Own Path​

Design open-ended systems where choices matter. Avoid systems that funnel players into dominant or expected playstyles. Players should define how they play, what they pursue, and what mastery means (without being locked into rigid identities).

Meaningful Progression​

Growth is earned, impactful, and felt. Progression should change how players think, act, and approach challenges (not just how strong they are). Increases in power must unlock new possibilities, not just reinforce existing ones.

Prioritize Experience​

Deliver meaningful play moments that respect the player's time and capacity to learn. Clarity should never come at the cost of discovery. Complexity should only exist where it creates new decisions or emotions.

Player Experience​

This defines what it feels like to play the game. It distills the central fantasy into a single statement, then expresses it through player action and emotional outcome.

I journey through shifting rifts where curiosity drives discovery, challenge forges mastery, and every return changes how I face the next journey.

Explore · Progress · Hone · Adapt

Curiosity · Accomplishment · Challenge · Awe
